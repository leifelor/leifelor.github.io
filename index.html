Foul Tarnished

Welcome to my site declaring the worst bosses to fight in Elden Ring!


Elden Ring is a game full of great bosses, from Starscourge Radahn to Godfrey. Unfortunately, with the game being so large, there's bound to be some stinkers. This site is dedicated to listing said stinkers, and more importantly, explaining why they suck to fight.


Tibia Mariner

This enemy is such a bore to fight. It's overly tanky and relies on summons and AOE attacks to deal damage. It overall isn't a fun fight because most of the time you're ignoring the summons and just chipping it's health down. It certainly isn't the worst fight in the world, there aren't any unfair attacks or pesky gimmicks to handle, it's just boring.


Magma Wyrms

These bosses, and yes there are multiple, are the opposite of boring to fight. They are easily one of the most annoying bosses to fight in the entire game. It's all because of one attack they have where they run across the entire arena then run across it again all while spewing maga underneath them. The magma lasts for way to long, and you stagger everytime you touch it, not to mention the insane amount of damage it does with just 2 ticks of damage. The boss overall is very slow, hard hitting, and very tanky, which would be fine if it weren't for that one attack that it uses constantly! Thankfully you aren't required to fight any of them to complete the game, however they do guard some nice loot behind their boss fights. I still hate these things though.


Valiant Gargoyles

This is getting out of hand, now there are two of them! That's the modo for this boss fight. As if fighting one of them wasn't bad enough, now you have to fight two of them in the same arena at the same time. They are both agro towards you at the same time meaning that you have to evade flurries of attacks from both of them at once. And if that wasn't bad enough, they both spit wide areas of poison that will drain your health overtime on top of the ridiculous damage they do with each attack. Managing one of them is fine, but two at once makes it difficult to get even one attack in, that's if you have enough stamina after all the rolling you have to do. This boss is extremely annoying, and if you want to do Ranni's questline to get her ending, you have to kill these guys every time.
